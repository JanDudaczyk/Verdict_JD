It is the feature.

of Americans say they regularly run into misinformation in conversations and on social media.

6x

Most people argue to win, not to find the truth. Fallacies go unchecked and conversations go in circles. The best debates are won by the most accurate voice, not the loudest.

How it works

Four steps.
One verdict.

01

Pick your format

Casual, Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Parliamentary, Policy, or Mock Trial.

02

Set the topic

Choose a preset or type your own.

03

Place and speak

Put the phone between you. It listens, transcribes, and flags issues live.

04

Get the verdict

Scores update live. At the end, an AI judge rules.

The team

JD
Jan Dudaczyk
Co-Founder & CTO

Based in the NYC metropolitan area. Jan has a background in software engineering, AI, and machine learning, and has taught Python, AI, and ML to students at local public libraries. He built Verdict Debate because he believes most arguments could be resolved if both sides were actually willing to listen, and saw technology as the only neutral party capable of holding them to that standard.
